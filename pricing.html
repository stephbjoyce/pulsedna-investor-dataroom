Sovereign & Verified Intelligence

A land-and-expand model built for regulated enterprises. Start with a low-friction pilot. Expand to full sovereign infrastructure. Every tier creates switching costs and deepens the moat.

Tier 1 — Magic Lens Pilot
Entry · Sandbox or Limited Evaluation · Days to Weeks
$50K – $100K/yr
Rapid value without infrastructure commitment. Designed for compliance officers, regulatory affairs leads, and engineering teams evaluating AI for the first time. No operational risk. No data leaves the boundary.
  • Magic Lens semantic search across regulatory document corpus
  • NRC ADAMS ingestion and citation retrieval
  • History & Evidence lens (RAG with confidence scores)
  • Basic audit trail and provenance logging
  • Sandbox environment — no production system integration required
  • Onboarding in days, full value in weeks
Tier 2 — Regulated Enterprise
Mid-Market · Full Platform · 10 CFR 50 Compliant

Full Magic Lens platform with all four intelligence lenses, Knowledge Graph, and compliance module. For organizations with active NRC licensing, PTAB proceedings, or FDA regulatory submissions.

  • Neo4j Knowledge Graph with regulatory relationship mapping
  • 10 CFR 50 Appendix B audit trail — Criterion XVI compliant
  • John Snow Labs integration for unstructured clinical/handwritten data (NLP)
  • FedRAMP-aligned security posture
  • Expert verification services (human-in-the-loop)
  • Optional Supermicro node for on-premise deployment
