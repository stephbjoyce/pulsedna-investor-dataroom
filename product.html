🏗️ What Magic Lens Actually Is

Magic Lens is a Federated AI Knowledge Intelligence Platform — a hybrid cloud/on-premise system that enables organizations to query, reason over, and extract verified insights from large, distributed, and heterogeneous document corpora without centralizing sensitive data.

The core architecture combines four intelligence lenses:

🔍 HISTORY & EVIDENCE LENS
Temporal reasoning over regulatory history. Surfaces precedent, prior art, and historical compliance decisions with citation chains.
💬 DISCOURSE & EXPERT LENS
Identifies expert consensus, dissenting opinions, and knowledge gaps across technical literature and regulatory submissions.
🕸️ KNOWLEDGE GRAPH LENS
Neo4j-powered relationship mapping between regulations, components, incidents, and personnel. Visualizes compliance dependency chains.
⚠️ ANOMALY DETECTION LENS
Real-time streaming anomaly detection via Kafka pipeline. Identifies deviations from regulatory baselines and operational norms.
